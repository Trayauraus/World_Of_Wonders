About the Game

A peaceful life shattered by the Black Plague. After watching his world be engulfed into darkness, and his family frozen into glowing white statues, our hero bunny finds himself at the edge of a cliff with a gloomy red glow below. With the plague right behind him, he has no choice but to jump. Navigate treacherous zones and avoid the infection at all costs.

Import Info

Take control of your experience! The Import System is located directly below the Play button in the main menu.

SUPPORTED FORMATS

.wowlv The official custom level format exported to the game.
.tlst Editor tileset saves for main tilesets and backgrounds.
.dat The main level data project file saved by the editor.

(Levels MUST be created in the WoW Editor)

Simply import your exported .wowlv files and dive into new worlds instantly.
